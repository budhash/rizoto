About Rizoto

Rizoto (resize + photo) gives any photo a new frame. Enter your output dimensions, then drag and zoom to find the perfect crop. Download an exact-size PNG, JPG, or WebP.

Your photos stay yours

Photos are processed in your browser. They are never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared. There are no accounts, analytics, or tracking scripts.

A few useful details

Use arrow keys to move your photo; hold Shift for bigger steps. Zoom starts at the minimum needed to fill your frame. Small photos can be enlarged, but enlarging cannot add detail. JPG exports use white behind transparent areas.

Supports 1–8192 pixels per side, up to 32 megapixels. Your browser must support the input image format; JPG, PNG, and WebP are good choices.
